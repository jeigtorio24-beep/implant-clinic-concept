How an implant is built up · general information
  1. 01 · Consultation

    First, a conversation.

    It usually starts with a talk and a check-up. The dentist looks at the gap, the teeth around it and the gums, and explains the options.

  2. 02 · Scan

    Then, a look inside.

    Imaging such as X-rays or a 3D scan shows the bone underneath and where a post could sit.

  3. 03 · Placement

    A titanium post goes in.

    A small threaded post is placed in the jawbone, and the bone is given time to heal around it.

  4. 04 · Crown

    Last, the crown.

    A connector called an abutment joins the post, and a crown made to match the nearby teeth is fixed on top.

Illustration and general information only. Every case is different, and the clinic will explain its own steps and timing at a consultation.

One gap, a few, or a whole arch.

Implants can hold one crown, a short bridge, or a full row of teeth. Choose a case to see how it generally works.

One missing tooth

A single implant post stands in for the root of the missing tooth, and one crown sits on top. The teeth on either side are left as they are.

  • One post, one abutment, one crown
  • Built up in the steps shown at the top of this page
Ask about one tooth

A few teeth in a row

Where several neighbouring teeth are missing, a short bridge can sometimes rest on two implants instead of one implant for every tooth.

  • Fewer posts than missing teeth
  • Whether it suits a case is decided after an exam and imaging
Ask about a few teeth

A full arch (All-on-4)

A full-arch restoration replaces a whole row of teeth with one fixed bridge held by a small number of implants. “All-on-4” is the name of a well-known way of doing this, with four implants in one arch, the back two often set at an angle.

  • One fixed bridge for the whole arch
  • Only a dentist can say whether it suits you

What is a dental implant?

In general terms, a small post, usually titanium, placed in the jawbone to stand in for a tooth root. An abutment joins it to a crown on top. The 3D illustration at the top of the page shows the parts.

Do you do All-on-4?

The clinic has posted about All-on-4. Whether it suits you can only be decided at a consultation, so please request one or call.
